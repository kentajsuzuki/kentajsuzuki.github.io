Seminar on Singular Support

The goal is to define the singular support of \(\ell\)-adic sheaves, explore its basic properties, and (time-admitting) give applications.
Organizers: Joakim Færgeman, Kenta Suzuki
When? Wednesdays 3:00-4:30PM, Fine Hall 1201

Schedule

  • September 9 (Joakim). Organization and Introduction
Sheaves on real manifolds
  • September 16 (Xiaoyu). Derived categories of sheaves
  • September 23 (Paweł). Six functors, nearby and vanishing cycles, and Fourier-Sato transform
  • September 30 (Xingzhu). Definition of singular support and basic properties Note the special time: 2:30–4:00PM, because of the annual reception.
Sheaves on complex manifolds
  • October 7 (Sam). \(\mathbb C\)-constructible sheaves and holomorphic nearby cycles
  • October 14 (Meenakshi). Singular support is Lagrangian, and characteristic cycles
  • October 21. Fall Recess -- no meeting!
  • October 28. Index formula
\(\ell\)-adic sheaves
  • November 4 (Dhruv). Sheaves on sites, \(\ell\)-adic sheaves, and six functors
  • November 11. Definition of singular support à la Beilinson
  • November 18. The characteristic cycle and singular support of \(\ell\)-adic sheaves
  • November 25. Thanksgiving Recess -- no meeting!
  • December 2. Characteristic cycles and Saito's index formula
  • December 9. the Grothendieck-Ogg-Shafarevich formula, and applications
