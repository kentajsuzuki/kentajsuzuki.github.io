Seminar on Singular Support

The goal is to define the singular support of \(\ell\)-adic sheaves, explore its basic properties, and (time-admitting) give applications.
Organizers: Joakim Faergeman, Kenta Suzuki
When? Wednesdays 3:00-4:30PM, Fine Hall 1201

Schedule

  • September 2 (Joakim). Organization and Introduction
Sheaves on real manifolds
  • September 9. Derived categories of sheaves
  • September 16. Six functors, nearby and vanishing cycles, and Fourier-Sato transform
  • September 23. Definition of singular support and basic properties
  • September 30. Characteristic cycles
Sheaves on complex manifolds
  • October 7. \(\mathbb C\)-constructible sheaves and holomorphic nearby cycles
  • October 14. Singular support via holomorphic nearby cycles
  • October 21. Fall Recess -- no meeting!
\(\ell\)-adic sheaves
  • October 28. Sheaves on sites, \(\ell\)-adic sheaves, and six functors
  • November 4. Constructible sheaves are holonomic
  • November 11. The characteristic cycle and singular support of \(\ell\)-adic sheaves
  • November 18. Saito's index formula
  • November 25. Thanksgiving Recess -- no meeting!
  • December 2. the Grothendieck-Ogg-Shafarevich formula, and applications
