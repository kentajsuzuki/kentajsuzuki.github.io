Mentoring

2026

  • For MIT's SPUR+ program, I am mentoring two students for a research project on the Gelfand-Tsetlin algebra in positive characteristic.
  • For the MIT PRIMES program, I am mentoring two research projects: one on relative Langlands and one on the Alexander polynomial.
  • For Princeton's DRP in the spring, I mentored a student on algebraic geometry.

2025

  • For the MIT PRIMES program, I mentored a research project on the representation theory of rational Cherednik algebras. My student Eddy won silver at Yau's competition!
  • For Princeton's DRP, I mentored three students on knot theory, enumerative combinatorics, and algebraic topology.
  • For RSI, I mentored two research projects: one on knot theory, and one on Temperley-Lieb algebras.
    • Sophia Jin, Coloring-Based Knot Invariants in Projective Space. She was top 300 in Regeneron's STS!
    • Rachel Chen, Explicit Irreducible Decomposition of the Spin Representation of the Temperley-Lieb Algebra. She was 4th place in Regeneron's STS!

2024

  • For the MIT PRIMES program, I mentored a project computing affine Kazhdan-Lusztig polynomials. Check out the final paper here!
