盗作 · 2020

花人局Flower and Badger Game

The title 花人局 is not a real Japanese word. It is a portmanteau of 花, which means flower, and 美人局, which means badger game–a sexual trap involving a woman. To keep the feel, I decided to translate 花人局 in the lyrics as “flower game.” Additionally, there is a pun–花人局 is pronounced the same as “handing me a flower” in the lyrics.

Leaving a goodbye and handing me a flower In a blur, the sky dawns I wake up in an empty room Only a hint of warmth

I don’t remember anything from last night, but Somebody was here, I just have a feeling Hungover, my cogs aren’t turning, but Eh it’s fine if I don’t know, that’s pretty too

Toothbrush by the mirror, somebody’s cup, moisturizer on the shelf, So much I don’t recall The pillow smells of flowers

Leaving a goodbye and handing me a flower In a blur, the sky dawns I wake up in an empty room Only a hint of warmth

I don’t remember anything from last night, but I suspect a badger game, just maybe My hangover has cleared, but it's fine if I don’t understand It’s better that way

Lavender blooms by the window, the sink is a mess, a half-knit scarf, So much I don’t recall The room smells of spring

Lost in the mist, a flower trap Nobody comes, and the sun sets The setting sun through a window I am alone, ignorant as ever

I actually remember a little from last night As life without you continues Even now, I keep thinking with this brain How you disappeared handing me a flower

You will probably return tomorrow Like nothing happened I can almost hear the door open You say, sorry I’m late I keep waiting for those words I keep waiting for the sunset
